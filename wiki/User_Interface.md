# User Interface & Controls

Korangar's UI is designed to feel like a modern MMO, moving away from the legacy click-modes of classic Ragnarok Online.

## 🛠️ The HUD & Action Bars
- **HUD Edit Mode:** You are not locked into a fixed layout. You can drag, scale, and lock almost every UI element (Action Bars, Minimap, Party Frames) to fit your playstyle. These profiles are saved per-character.
- **Action Bars:** Your primary way of interacting in combat. They feature modern keybind overlays and smooth cooldown animations so you don't have to guess when a skill is ready.
- **Cast & Buff Bars:** Cast bars will show you when an enemy or player is channeling a skill, and whether it can be interrupted. Buffs and debuffs appear with duration timers, and you can right-click your own buffs to cancel them.

## ⚔️ Skills & Skill Tree
The skill tree has been heavily modernized:
- **Tabs:** Skills are organized cleanly into tabs rather than a single massive scrolling list.
- **Adding Points:** You can allocate your available skill points directly within this window by clicking the upgrade buttons next to a skill. 
- **Equipping Skills:** To use an active skill, simply **click and drag** it from the skill tree directly onto one of your Action Bar slots.
- *(Coming Soon)*: A search/filter bar and a **Build Planner** to let you map out your progression before committing points!

## 🖱️ Interaction & Drag-and-Drop
Korangar relies on modern interaction primitives rather than awkward legacy modes:
- **Drag-and-Drop:** You can drag skills, items from your inventory, and equipment intuitively across windows. 
- **Context Menus:** Right-clicking on players, items, or skills will open a clean context menu (similar to WoW or FFXIV) for quick actions like inspecting, trading, or viewing detailed info.
- **Smart Tooltips:** Hovering over an item or skill provides a detailed breakdown of its stats, and will automatically compare it to your currently equipped gear.
