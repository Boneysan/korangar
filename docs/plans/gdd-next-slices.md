## Decisions confirmed for this friends server

- Non-story persistent discoveries and general access unlocks belong to the **account**, not one character: encountered monsters, visited places, rumors, and general services use the same account identity. **Story quests, story chapter completion, choices, and story-gated access remain per character; every character must complete the story.** Character level/build/inventory also remain character state. A different account keeps its own general unlock record. S7 implements monster milestones and map visits first; each later unlock-bearing feature must classify itself as general/account or story/character.
- DM Session mode stores quest/flag state on each character. A character joining the active campaign party automatically receives the party's current campaign quests and story flags, without past rewards; a same-account alternate receives them only after joining. Disconnected party members catch up on return. See [DM party quest-sync contract](../specs/dm-party-quest-sync.md) and S10.
- The player Adventure Guide defaults to an **open, broadly searchable reference**. Monster, item, card, skill, job, map, and mechanics entries—and verified build-relevant details such as effects, requirements, drops, and rates—are available without a kill or lore check. An unlock adds discovery history, badges, and optional authored encounter notes; it does not hide mechanical facts. Campaign plot spoilers and DM-only controls remain separate.
- Skill and stat points can both be reset by ordinary players, **free at any level**, so experimentation is low-friction. These are target policies, not claims that both controls currently work. Keep single-skill refund subject to the existing prerequisite safety rules.
