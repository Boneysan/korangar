## Quest sharing rule

Late-joining party members automatically inherit campaign quests and story progress, as decided 2026-09-23. This behavior must still pass the multiplayer checks above; the spec now records the same rule.

## Quests

Logging in or changing maps, while you are in the active DM party, copies quest progress and story flags from an online party member when yours are still empty. It does not overwrite a choice you already made. The same progress is stored on the party in `dm_campaign_party_state`. A later login can restore it even when nobody else is online. The first login of someone who already has the progress writes that store. `@dm reset confirm` clears it. Campaign quest entries no longer publish kill or drop objectives, so the log does not ask for Plankton kills that Wynne does not accept. The turn-in is the item list in `DM_HuntSpec`. The whole party's stacks are added together, including logged-out members. Items are taken only when that total is enough, and a short hand-in takes nothing. A logged-out member loses those items from their saved inventory. Equipped gear is not taken.
